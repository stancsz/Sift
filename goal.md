# Goal: make Sift useful, credible, and ready to adopt

## Status: active

Continue the user's full Sift improvement and release-readiness objective. The local testing and short-CI design, first-screen value, public Pages site, expanded LongBench report, and LiteLLM-free CLI are substantially implemented. Do not claim broad adoption, general agent quality, or stable readiness from those artifacts alone. Completion requires credible held-out quality evidence, usable first-run evidence, compatible clients, verified publishing controls, and a successful stable release.

## Current constraint

The product now demonstrates a measurable token/quality trade-off, but the quality evidence uses development-exposed LongBench questions and the first-run flow has no external users yet. The 344-question, three-repeat study showed 59.66% fewer provider prompt tokens while Sift scored 5.25 percentage points lower on task-majority exact choice; the task-cluster 95% interval was −10.20 to −0.29 pp. User trust and successful agent-task outcomes remain unproven. The prior study's cap and its unreceipted-attempt reserve remain in that study's accounting. On 2026-10-20 the user separately authorized up to $100 for a fresh 344-question × 3-repeat-per-arm MiniMax run. The fresh-run wrapper models about $41.87 for the new schedule and supports the user's explicit assumption if prior reports cannot be found, while preserving the tracked 13 unreceipted attempts and $2.0457216 reserve. The user has confirmed there is no other active or unsettled MiniMax/LongBench request; immediately before any future dispatch, refresh that attestation, the route, usage, and the reserve. Dispatch remains paused until MiniMax billing coverage is verified. The client cap is not a provider-enforced invoice ceiling.
